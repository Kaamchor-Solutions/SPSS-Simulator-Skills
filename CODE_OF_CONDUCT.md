# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Critique ideas, analyses, and code—not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when mistakes occur, and learn from feedback.
- Respect privacy; do not post identifiable or sensitive data in issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, threats, sustained disruption, and publishing another person’s private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. They will act proportionately and explain enforcement decisions when practical.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub’s private contact mechanisms. Do not report conduct concerns in public issues if doing so could expose someone to harm.

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community code; see https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ for the full reference text and license.
